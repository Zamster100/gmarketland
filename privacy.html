1 Overview

GoMarket ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information about you when you use the GoMarket platform, including during its closed beta period.

GoMarket is non-custodial — we never hold your funds or control your private keys. However, we do collect limited information to operate the platform and improve the product.

By using GoMarket, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Type Examples How collected
Account information Email address (if provided via waitlist) Directly from you
Wallet data Public wallet address used to connect Via wallet connection
Usage data Pages visited, clicks, session duration, feature interactions Automatically via logs
Device & browser Browser type, OS, screen resolution, IP address Automatically via logs
Beta feedback Bug reports, survey responses, written feedback Directly from you

We do not collect government IDs, financial account numbers, or other sensitive personal data during the beta phase.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the GoMarket platform.
  • Manage beta access, including invitations and waitlist processing.
  • Send you important updates about the beta program, product changes, and your account (where you have provided an email address).
  • Analyze usage patterns to identify bugs, improve user experience, and prioritize features.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or prohibited activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your email address to send unsolicited marketing communications without your explicit consent.

4 Blockchain & On-Chain Data

GoMarket operates on public blockchain infrastructure. You should be aware that:

  • All transactions executed on-chain are permanently recorded on a public blockchain and are visible to anyone.
  • Your wallet address and transaction history are public by nature of the blockchain — we have no ability to make these private.
  • We do not associate your wallet address with your email address or identity without your consent.
If privacy is a concern, consider using a dedicated wallet address for your GoMarket activity that is not linked to your personal identity.

5 Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers: Third-party vendors who assist us with hosting, analytics, email delivery, and security. These providers are contractually required to keep your information confidential.
  • Legal authorities: Where required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of GoMarket or others.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

In all other cases, we will not share your information without your explicit consent.

6 Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Usage logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Beta feedback is retained indefinitely in anonymised or aggregated form.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@gomarket.io. We will process your request within 30 days.

7 Cookies & Tracking

GoMarket uses minimal cookies and local storage to:

  • Maintain your session and preferences.
  • Understand how users navigate the platform (analytics).

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the platform may not function correctly as a result.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing, including marketing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@gomarket.io. We do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

9 Children's Privacy

GoMarket is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@gomarket.io.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Where we hold your email address, we will notify you of significant changes directly.

Your continued use of the Service after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

11 Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@gomarket.io
  • For data deletion requests: Include "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line.

We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 5 business days.
